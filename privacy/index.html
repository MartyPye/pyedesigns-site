Privacy policy

Your data stays with you

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy covers SwingJournal, the tennis journal app made by Pye Designs LLC ("Pye Designs", "we"), and this website. The short version: we don't collect anything.

SwingJournal

What we collect

Nothing. SwingJournal has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Pye Designs never receives your journal, your players, your notes or anything about how you use the app.

Where your journal is stored

Everything you enter (players, photos, matches, practices, notes, voice memos and reviews) is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it's also kept in your own private iCloud database so it syncs to your other iPhone and iPad. Only your Apple Account can read that database; Pye Designs can't. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

If iCloud is off, your journal stays on that device only.

Contacts and photos

When you add a player from Contacts or link a contact, you pick one card in Apple's contact picker, and SwingJournal saves only that card's name, phone number and photo with the player. The app never reads the rest of your contacts and doesn't ask for Contacts access. Photos you choose for players are saved with your journal in the same way.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you record a voice memo, SwingJournal asks for the microphone. Recordings are saved with your journal as described above. Turning a memo into text happens on your device; the audio isn't sent anywhere to be transcribed.

Notifications

Review reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us to deliver them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Pye Designs doesn't see your payment details; the app only learns whether your subscription is active.

Exports

When you export your journal as JSON or CSV, the file goes wherever you choose to share or save it. That's up to you.

Children

SwingJournal is meant for players of all ages, including juniors, and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete players or sessions in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage and delete SwingJournal's data.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, ads or forms. Like any web host, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We don't use them to identify you. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: support@pyedesigns.com.
