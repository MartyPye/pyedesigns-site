Common questions

Where is my journal stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud if you're signed in, so it syncs between your iPhone and iPad. We can't see it.

How do I move my journal to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for SwingJournal. Your journal syncs down on its own.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose SwingJournal. Apple handles all billing.

I subscribed but the app still shows the free limits.

In SwingJournal, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. In SwingJournal's Settings, choose Export as JSON or Export as CSV. Export always works, with or without a subscription.
