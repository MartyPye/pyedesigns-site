SwingJournal

Your private tennis journal. Players, matches and practices, with prep before and review after.

Know every opponent

Search a name and see your head-to-head, every match and score, their strengths, weaknesses and rating.

Prep before you play

Plan A and Plan B, serve and return strategy, cue words and a warm-up, with what you already know about your opponent.

Review after

How the plan worked, what went well, what to improve and the lessons to carry into the next match.

Structured practice

Objectives, drills from your own library and a rating for each drill, so practice has a point.

Quick notes at the court

Jot a note or record a voice memo between sets, then write the full review at home.

Private by design

No account and no servers. Your journal stays on your devices and syncs through your own iCloud.
